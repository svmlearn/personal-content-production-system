# Search Strategy

This strategy captures the current BOSS job-search approach as of 2026-06-09. Update it when new user feedback changes what counts as a good AI product-manager role.

## Core Correction

Do not use `大模型产品经理` as the main search term. It is useful only as a supplemental term because it over-recalls financial large-model roles, voice/audio, data governance, model evaluation, algorithm strategy, vehicle/robotics, and engineering-heavy jobs.

Use `AI产品经理` as the main pool-building term, then split search work across Agent, AI Native, AI application, AI tool, workflow, AIGC, marketing, and ecommerce terms.

## Collection Rules

- Build a broad list-level pool first. A 100+ raw pool is acceptable; only detail-collect the promising subset for Feishu.
- When opening BOSS detail pages, work in small batches, usually 3-5 tabs at a time. After each batch is extracted and saved, close those tabs before opening more.
- For each primary keyword, check at least the first 2 pages when BOSS allows it.
- Prefer Shenzhen first. Expand to Guangzhou, Hangzhou, Shanghai, and Beijing only for strong AI Agent / AI Native / AI tool / AI marketing / ecommerce-AI matches.
- Do not hard-filter salary at `18-30K`. Keep `15-60K` in the list pool, then prioritize `15-35K`, `18-35K`, and `20-40K` in review.
- Prefer `1-3年`, `3-5年`, and `经验不限`. Keep `5-10年` only when the JD is highly aligned and not a director/manager-heavy role.
- Do not over-filter company size during collection. Use company size, financing, registered capital, and address during `投递意愿` scoring.
- Separate list-level candidates from detail-collected Feishu rows. Do not write shallow list-only candidates into Feishu as if JD/company details were verified.

## Fast List-Level Downgrades

Usually skip or mark `无/低` before opening details when the list signal shows:

- Engineering, algorithm, development, R&D lead, technical director, or implementation lead as the main role.
- Voice/audio, data governance, vehicle, robotics, smart hardware, IoT, consumer electronics, medical device, or wearable hardware as the main product context.
- Internship or day-rate roles unless specifically useful as market intelligence.
- `10年以上`, director, head, partner, or founder-like responsibilities.
- Anonymous headhunter plus vague title and missing company clues.
- Web3, OTC, trading, payment, or risk-control as the main domain.

## Detail-Level Scoring

Give `高` only when most of these are true:

- Core product is AI Agent, AI Native, AI application, AI tools, SaaS/PaaS, workflow, knowledge base, automation, AIGC, AI marketing, or ecommerce-growth tooling.
- JD emphasizes productization, 0-to-1, user workflows, PRD/prototyping, metrics, iteration, and cross-functional delivery.
- The user's portfolio can be naturally pitched: content marketing system, Agent projects, AI automation, ecommerce/content growth, and deep AI coding-tool usage.
- Company facts are reasonably complete: business, size, address, registered capital or public entity info, and benefits.
- Requirements do not hinge on algorithm engineering, model training, hard ML frameworks, deep vertical domain expertise, or hardware production.

Use `中` for plausible matches with one or two meaningful risks. Use `低` for backup/reference roles that are possible but not worth customization. Use `无` for obvious mismatch or serious information gaps.
